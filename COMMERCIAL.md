# Commercial / institutional seats

The MIT core of `fw-ck-report` stays free for individuals and teaching.

**Institutional seat:** commercial use of `fw-ck-report` in a named project, with priority triage. Core CLI stays MIT for individuals and teaching.

Included with a seat:
- Priority issue triage within an agreed window
- One written judgment receipt on whether your CK / inhibition table is report-ready

Not included:
- Custom pipelines, private forks, or “run the analysis for me” ops
- Unpaid consulting hours

## Pricing

- **Lab seat:** ~€490 / seat / year (Datanovia Teams anchor)
- **How to buy:** open a GitHub Issue with label intent `seat-inquiry` — [new issue](https://github.com/Mingdavid001/fw-ck-report/issues/new). CNY / EUR parallel on invoice.
